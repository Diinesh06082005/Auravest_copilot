### Scene 2: How to Search Tickers (0:35 – 1:15)
**Screen Action**:
1. Hover over the top navigation bar or press `Ctrl + K` to open the Command Palette search overlay.
2. Type `NVDA` (or `NVIDIA Corporation`).
3. Point out the command option: **"Run AI Research Workflow for NVDA"**.
4. Press `Enter` or click the option.

**What to Say (Speaker Script)**:

### Scene 3: How It Works — Multi-Agent Research & Live Streaming (1:15 – 2:15)
**Screen Action**:
1. Highlight the **Dynamic Island** pill floating at the top center of the screen as it pulses blue and displays percentage progress (`AI Agent: Researching NVDA... 45%`).
2. Show the split loading screen featuring real-time neon stock price chart animations on one side and stock news feeds on the other.

**What to Say (Speaker Script)**:
> *"Here is where AuraVest's underlying technology shines. Instead of sending a single prompt to a generic chatbot which can hallucinate or time out, AuraVest executes a stateful multi-agent graph powered by LangGraph.*
>
> *Look at the top center — our Dynamic Island streams progress in real time via Server-Sent Events (SSE). Each agent node handles a dedicated responsibility:*
> * *Node 1 validates SEC ticker data.*
> * *Node 2 audits balance sheets and income statements.*
> * *Node 3 parses real-time news headlines using Tavily Search.*
> * *Node 4 runs our custom Machine Learning financial risk model.*

### Scene 4: Exploring the AI Dossier & Dual-AI Architecture (2:15 – 3:30)
**Screen Action**:
1. Dashboard auto-scrolls to the top once research completes.
2. Hover over the **Consensus Rating Card** (e.g. `BUY - 92% Confidence`).
3. Scroll through the **Quant Health Score Badge** (`Grade: A+`, `Quant Score: 94/100`, `Bankruptcy Probability: 1.2%`).
4. Point to the interactive **Radar Chart** (Financial Health, Valuation, Growth, Volatility, Sentiment).
5. Scroll down to show **YouTube Video Intelligence**, **SWOT Matrix**, and **Bull/Bear Catalysts**.

**What to Say (Speaker Script)**:
> *"Once compiled, AuraVest delivers a comprehensive institutional dossier.*
>
> *Our system operates on a **Dual-AI Architecture**:*
> 1. *First, our custom ML Model — `Auravest-FinQuant` — provides mathematical precision, scoring the stock's Quant Health, letter grade, and bankruptcy risk percentage based on balance sheet metrics.*
> 2. *Second, our LangGraph LLM agents provide high-level narrative analysis — generating an overall BUY/SELL consensus rating, key catalyst thesis, SWOT matrix, and real-time market sentiment.*
>
> *We also integrate YouTube video intelligence in the sidebar so analysts can watch top market commentary without leaving the dashboard."*
